An open financial model of the AI build-out

AI economics

What does a dollar of GPU compute earn, who captures it, and does the supply being built match the demand being guided to?

The model follows the three layers that split that dollar: the chip makers who sell the GPUs, the neoclouds who rent them out by the hour, and the hyperscalers who buy most of them and guide the demand.

Every reported figure traces to an SEC filing; every assumption is listed on the Inputs sheet with its source. Every writeup ends with a position and the figure that would prove it wrong.
